Scoring Methodology & Mathematical Formulation

The FACEGUARD AI Risk Assessment Score is an explainable linear composite index: $$\text{Score} = w_{\text{CNN}} \cdot S_{\text{CNN}} + w_{\text{Sensor}} \cdot S_{\text{Sensor}} + w_{\text{FFT}} \cdot S_{\text{FFT}} + w_{\text{ELA}} \cdot S_{\text{ELA}} + w_{\text{EXIF}} \cdot S_{\text{EXIF}}$$ where weights satisfy \(\sum w_i = 100\).

Crucial Academic Principle: Risk Score measures investigative review priority, not simple posterior probability. Raw convolutional neural network softmax scores are inherently uncalibrated on out-of-distribution inputs. By combining orthogonal physical domains (Laplacian sensor noise, Fourier lattice grids, and ELA deltas), the system prevents correlated double-counting while surfacing actionable explanation vectors.
